# Organizer Toolkit

Welcome to the Critical Mass Organizer Toolkit. Whether you're planning your very first ride or maintaining a years-long tradition, this toolkit gives you the practical resources and guidance you need.

## What's inside

- **[First Ride Checklist](/toolkit/first-ride-checklist)** — A step-by-step guide to launching your first ride
- **[Route Planning](/toolkit/route-planning)** — How to choose safe, fun, inclusive routes
- **[Ride Planning & Operations](/toolkit/ride-planning-and-operations)** — Logistics for ride day
- **[Messaging & Outreach](/toolkit/messaging-and-outreach)** — Getting the word out and building community
- **[Graphics & Materials](/toolkit/graphics-and-materials)** — Flyers, spoke cards, social templates, and more
- **[Growth & Continuity](/toolkit/growth-and-continuity)** — Sustaining your ride long-term
- **[FAQ](/toolkit/faq)** — Common questions from organizers and riders
- **[Case Studies](/toolkit/case-studies)** — How other cities built their rides

## Who is this for?

This toolkit is for anyone who wants to organize or help sustain a Critical Mass ride. You don't need permission, a formal organization, or prior experience. Critical Mass is a decentralized movement — all you need is a time, a place, and people who want to ride.
