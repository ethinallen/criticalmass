## Design guidelines

If you're creating your own materials:

- **Keep it simple** — date, time, meeting point, and a clear "Critical Mass" label
- **Use high contrast** — your flyer needs to be readable from a distance and in low light
- **Include a QR code** linking to your social media or community page
- **Avoid cluttered designs** — white space is your friend
- **Use a readable font** — at least 14pt for body text on printed materials

## Printing tips

- Standard flyers: US Letter (8.5 x 11") or A4
- Spoke cards: 2.5 x 3.5" (business card size) on heavy card stock
- Many local print shops offer cheap short-run prints
- For spoke cards, consider lamination or heavy stock for weather resistance
