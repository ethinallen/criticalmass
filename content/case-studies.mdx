# Case Studies

Every city's Critical Mass is different. These case studies highlight how rides in different cities started, grew, and adapted to their local context.

## Coming soon

We're collecting stories from Critical Mass communities around the world. Case studies will cover:

- **How it started** — the origins of the ride in each city
- **What worked** — tactics and decisions that helped the ride grow
- **Challenges** — obstacles the community faced and how they overcame them
- **Lessons learned** — advice for organizers in other cities

## Want to contribute?

If you organize or have organized a Critical Mass ride and want to share your city's story, we'd love to hear from you. Reach out via the project's GitHub repository.

## Cities we're documenting

- San Francisco, USA — where it all began in 1992
- Berlin, Germany — one of the largest rides in Europe
- Budapest, Hungary — a ride known for massive turnout
- Chicago, USA — a long-running tradition on the lakefront
- São Paulo, Brazil — a major ride in South America
- London, UK — decades of riding through central London
- Melbourne, Australia — a vibrant Southern Hemisphere ride
